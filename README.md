# Preface
The facist threat in the United States and Biden's foreign policy add to concerns both about the future of whatever is left of American democracy and that of the world at large. However, "optimism over despair" has been one of Noam Chomsky's mottos, and thus he contends that humanity can avert a climate catastrophe and a nuclear holocaust. He is 95 years old and still stresses with the same conviction that activism is key to building a better world. 

# Just Transition Can Stop Earth From Becoming Uninhabitable: Noam Chomsky and Rober Pollin - June 7, 2023
It has been clear for decades that human activities are having an impact on the physical environment in many way and that we are the cause of global warming. It is true that some concrete actions have been taken over the past 3 decades or so to stop environmental degredation and reduce carbon emissions, but the gap between what is happening to the planet, which includes a sharp decline in biodiversity, and what is needed in terms of environmental and climate action seems to be growing rather than decreasing. Our handling of the climate is flawed, as evidenced by the growing emphasis on carbon capture technologies rather than doing away with fossil fuels. Another example is deforestation.
