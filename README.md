## How To Fall In Love With The Process Of Becoming Great
## Joshua Medcalf

### Chapter 1 -- Chop Wood, Carry Water
John and Joshua love samurai culture and traveling through Japan. Their favorite samurai warriors are archers.
John reads Joshua samurai stories and falls asleep to Jackie Chan movies in the hospital after a bad car accident.
After Joshua's recovery, John travels to Japan and joins a community of samurai to become a samurai archer.
His sensei is named Akira.
Akira's first task for apprentices is to chop wood and carry water because the community does not have heat and running water. They burned wood for heat in cold weather and brought in water from the well by hand for the bathroom and kitchen.
"Shooting will come soon enough. But first you must chop wood, and carry water." --Akira-sensei

### Chapter 2 -- Building Your Own House
After a day of chopping wood and carrying water, Akira told John a story. In Japan they have a deep love of architecture, and there once was a man named Kota who built some of the finest houses in all of Tokyo. His work became world famous due to his dedication to the process, his willingness to beat on his craft, and his relentless devotion to keep learning, even late into his career. Eventually he grew tired of building homes for other people and was ready to retire. After 30 years he wanted to move on and travel and spend time with family. He turned in his 2-week notice and his boss asked him to build one more house for an important client. He had a day to talk it over with his wife and then agreed. He agreed with his head but not with his heart. He viewed it more as an obligation than an opportunity. He had always been hands on but this time he delegated tasks and things slipped through the cracks. It was up to code, but it lacked the "wow" factor his other homes were known for.
