### Just Transition Can Stop Earth From Becoming Uninhabitable: Noam Chomsky and Rober Pollin - June 7, 2023
It has been clear for decades that human activities are having an impact on the physical environment in many way and that we are the cause of global warming. It is true that some concrete actions have been taken over the past 3 decades or so to stop environmental degredation and reduce carbon emissions, but the gap between what is happening to the planet, which includes a sharp decline in biodiversity, and what is needed in terms of environmental and climate action seems to be growing rather than decreasing. Our handling of the climate is flawed, as evidenced by the growing emphasis on carbon capture technologies rather than doing away with fossil fuels. Another example is deforestation. 

Fifty percent of the reductions we have to make to get near zero emissions by 2050 are going to come from technologies we don't yet have.

During Bolsonaro's term in Brazil, agribuisiness, mining, and logging enterprises were unleashed in an assualt on the forest and the Indigenous societies that have long lived in harmony with nature. "Deforestation across Brazil soared between 2019 and 2022 under the then presiden, Jair Bolsonaro, with cattle ranching being the number one cause." More than 800 million trees were destroyed for beef export.
Lula da Silva's election in 2022 offered hope to limit, perhaps end, the destruction. As minister of the environment, he appointed Marina Sila, an environmentalist. But "the masters of mankind" who own the economy (in Adam Smith's phrase) never rest. Their congressional supporters are chipping away at Silva's jurisdiction.
China is far in the lead globally in producting batteries, solar panels, and other key ingredients of the energy transition as summarized in the Washington Post. The US is very far behind in per capita terms. China is "likely on track to meet its goals of peaking its emissions before 2030 and achieving net-zero emissions by 2060. It installed a record number of solar power capacity last year and this year alone is set to install more than the entire existing solar capacity of the US." 
The US is seeking ways to pressure China to hep avert climate catastrophe. It warns that China is responsible for more than double US emissions.
Commerce Secretary Gina Raimondo said Tuesday that the US will rally allies in order to mount pressure on the world's second-largest economy. "If we really want to slow down China's rate of innovation, we need to work with Europe," Raimondo said. They prime method is to deny access to the computer chips necessary for advanced technology.

According to Chomsky, the education of women will help reduce the global population in a humane way. It has been shown to lead to sharp population reduction in both rich and poor countries. 

Phasing out fossil fuel consumption is beneficial on environmental grounds but it is still a job killer. Everyone other than the rich coastal elites care more about jobs than the environment.
